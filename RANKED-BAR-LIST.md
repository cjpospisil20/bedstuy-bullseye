## Caveats

- **My list is built from published guides, and those go stale.** Two of the
  original top six were closed. Confirm a bar is open before you travel to it.
- Space scores are **inferred from written descriptions**, not measured.
- Coordinates are **hand-estimated from street addresses** (~1–2 block accuracy).
- Boundary positions are traced from your screenshot against its scale bar —
  good to roughly a block. The ⚠️edge bars need your own check.
- "Tuesday clear" means **nothing recurring was found published**. It is not the
  same as the bar confirming it.
