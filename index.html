The venue test is therefore simple: can you walk to both the G and the C from the front door?

Branded Saloon’s Tuesday is confirmed free. Their public calendar holds 4,312 events going back to 2010, and Tuesday is comfortably their quietest night — two Tuesday bookings in all of 2026 against 24 Sundays and 16 Mondays, with nothing upcoming. Karaoke and trivia run Thursdays, the drag dinner Fridays.

Only one candidate has a room anyone has actually stood in. The owner of Moot Bar reports that Fulton Grand has a back room formerly used for shuffleboard or pinball — “would be perfect for darts if that were feasible.” A shuffleboard table is twenty-two feet long, so that room is unambiguously deep enough for two regulation lanes. Everything else here is inference. The open question at Fulton Grand is not whether the room fits — it is whether it is still free.
