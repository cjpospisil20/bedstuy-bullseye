Net effect: the Myrtle Ave corridor and the Fulton St corridor in Bed-Stuy are
both spoken for. The remaining white space is the **band between them** —
Gates Ave / Lafayette Ave / Bedford Ave / Tompkins Ave — which is also the
highest-scoring transit zone in the model (candidate C). Convenient.
